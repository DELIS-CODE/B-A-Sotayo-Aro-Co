MERCY OLUWAKOREDE AKINKUADE

Mercy Oluwakorede Akinkuade, Senior Associate. With extensive experience in *Civil Litigation, Criminal Defense, Land & Property Law, Labour & Employment Law, and Family Law*, Mercy Oluwakorede Akinkuade provides strategic counsel to individuals, families, and businesses navigating complex legal challenges. She is particularly recognized for *promoting Alternative Dispute Resolution* as a faster, cost-effective, and relationship-preserving way to resolve disputes. Where litigation is necessary, she represents clients with diligence before courts and tribunals across Nigeria. At B. A. Sotayo-Aro & Co., Mercy leads with integrity, confidentiality, and a client-first approach. Her goal is simple: to protect your rights, resolve your problems, and help you move forward with confidence.
